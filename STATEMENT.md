# FoodRescue

# Problem Statement

Large quantities of edible surplus food from restaurants, hotels, catering services, and events are wasted every day. At the same time, NGOs, shelters, and relief organizations may experience food shortages.

When multiple food donations and food requests exist simultaneously, it becomes difficult to determine which food should be allocated first and which receiver should receive it. Factors such as food expiry time, available quantity, receiver demand, and emergency level must be considered.

Manual coordination can cause delays, resulting in food being wasted while genuine food requirements remain unmet.

# Proposed Solution

FoodRescue is a Java-based command-line application designed to manage surplus food donations and food requests. The system tracks available food, monitors expiry time, manages receiver requirements, and prioritizes suitable receivers based on factors such as demand and emergency level.

The system helps recommend and manage food allocation to reduce food waste and improve the redistribution of surplus edible food.

# Target Users

- Restaurants and hotels
- Catering services
- NGOs
- Shelters
- Relief organizations
- Food redistribution coordinators

# High-Level Features

- Donor and surplus food management
- Receiver and food request management
- Food expiry tracking
- Receiver priority calculation
- Intelligent food matching and allocation
- Reporting and record management
